Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas: secondnaturemusic.app@gmail.com. It's a one-person app, so you'll get a reply from an actual human — usually within two or three days.

If you're reporting a bug, it helps enormously to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the piece's length, and what you were doing when it happened. Mneme has no analytics, so your description is genuinely the only information I get.

Common questions

Where do I start with something new?

Add it — paste it, use Find online, share it from Safari, open a PDF, Word or text file, or pick screenshots or scan a printed sheet — choose whether it's a song, a script or a poem or other text, and check the section cards Mneme shows before you save. Then open it and tap the mode marked Up next. For a new piece that's Learn: one section at a time, sung or said out loud, with the words fading as each one holds.

It marked a line wrong that I sang right.

Speech recognition can mishear, and singing is harder for it than speech. Mneme is built to say "not heard" rather than guess, so a line it really couldn't make out is shown as unheard and never counts against you. If clean lines keep being marked wrong, tell me which piece and which words — that is exactly the case I most want to fix.

Can I sing along with a guitar or a backing track?

You can, but the microphone hears less of your voice over an instrument. A pass Mneme barely heard doesn't count either way — it can't promote a song or mark one down. For the clearest results, sing unaccompanied, or hold the phone nearer your mouth than the instrument. Speaking the words works best of all.

What do Ready and Solid mean?

A piece is Ready when you've sung or said it through from the top, with nothing on screen, cleanly. It's Solid when you can also pick it up cold from any section — which is what saves you when you lose your place on stage.

How many pieces are free?

Three songs, scripts or poems, with every mode, for as long as you like. Mneme Pro is a one-time purchase that lets you add as many as you want. Deleting a piece makes room for another.

I bought Pro and it isn't showing.

Open Settings › Mneme Pro › Restore purchases. Your purchase follows your Apple Account, so it works on a new phone or after a reinstall.

How do I set up a page-turner pedal?

Most pedals type keys and need no setup. Pair it in the iPhone's Settings › Bluetooth, open a piece's Stage, and press: → ↓, Page Down, Space and Return turn forward, or start the scroll; ← ↑ and Page Up turn back.

A MIDI pedal (an M-VAVE Chocolate in its MIDI mode, for one) sends messages instead. In Mneme's Settings › Page-turner pedal, connect it, then tap Forward and press the button you want for it, and the same for Back. Program changes, control changes and notes all work. Set the pedal's buttons to momentary, not toggle, or every other press is lost.

How do I learn my part in a script?

Add the script as a Script. A screenplay or a stage play is read into characters and scenes when you tap Next; check what it found, fix any name it got wrong from a line's menu, and say whose part is yours. Everyone else's lines stay up as your cues and are never judged. In Learn and Test, Show their lines chooses how many: all of them, only the one before each of yours, or none.

What about a poem, a speech or scripture?

Add it as a Poem or text and speak it. Spoken words are what the recognizer hears best.
