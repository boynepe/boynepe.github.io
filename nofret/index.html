Nofret

Guitar note trainer for iPhone

You don't know the fretboard until you don't have to think about it. Nofret drills you toward sub-second recall in five minutes a day — on iPhone.

It works in both directions: name the note at a position you're shown, and find a note you're told on the neck. It measures how fast you answer, not just whether you were right, because speed is the whole point. There's no account, no sign-in, and nothing to set up.

Turn the phone sideways for twice as much neck, or keep it upright, whichever you read faster. Put the thinnest string on top, like tilting the neck up to see it, or the thickest, like the strings in your hands — and mirror the board for a left-handed guitar. And if you're new to the fretboard, Study is there before anything is timed: learn the basics, see every note and the patterns they make, and get tips for memorizing them.

The first two stages are free, forever. The rest is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
