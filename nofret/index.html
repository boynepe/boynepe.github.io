Nofret

Learn the guitar fretboard

You don't know the fretboard until you don't have to think about it. Nofret drills you toward sub-second recall in ninety-second sessions — on iPhone.

It works in both directions: name the note at a position, and find every position of a note. It measures how fast you answer, not just whether you were right, because speed is the whole point. There's no account, no sign-in, and nothing to set up.
