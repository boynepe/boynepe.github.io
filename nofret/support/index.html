Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas: nofret.app@gmail.com. It's a one-person app, so you'll get a reply from an actual human — usually within two or three days.

If you're reporting a bug, it helps enormously to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Nofret has no analytics, so your description is genuinely the only information I get.

Common questions

Why won't it let me move to the next stage?

Moving up takes three things at once: 90% accuracy, 85% of the stage answered at speed, and both on two separate days. You also have to meet them in both directions — finding notes and naming them. The second day is deliberate: a night's sleep is part of how the previous stage sticks.

If you'd rather not wait, Settings › Progress bypass › Allow same-day promotion removes the cross-day requirement. That section is labeled "not recommended" for the reason above, but it's your app and your call.

Is there a countdown timer? I don't want to be rushed.

No. Nothing in Nofret is on a clock — no mode has a countdown, and you're never marked wrong for taking your time. A session is a fixed number of questions and ends when you've answered them.

Nofret does measure how long you take, because fast recall is the skill it's training, but measuring is not the same as pressuring. If a session still feels like too much, Free Practice, New String, Weak Spots and Find Them All don't count toward stage progression at all — they're for exploring the neck without anything at stake.

What was that speed check before my first drill?

It measures how long you take to tap or say a note you've already been given — pure reaction and movement, with no thinking involved. That figure is then subtracted from every drill answer, so what Nofret reports is how long you took to identify the note, not how fast your thumb is. It's why the times are comparable between a phone in your hand and a phone on a table.

You can see and reset each measurement in Settings › Response time. Resetting one just means the quick check runs again before your next drill that needs it.

I already know the fretboard. Do I have to start at the beginning?

No. The placement test at first launch should put you well up the ladder. If it got it wrong, Settings › Progress bypass › Change stage lets you go anywhere.

Answering by voice isn't recognizing me.

Start with the ordinary things: use it somewhere quiet, and say the note deliberately rather than quickly. Recognition is noticeably better on iOS 26 and later, and if it keeps mishearing you, tapping is just as good — nothing is scored differently.

Plain note letters are usually handled better than notes with a sharp or a flat, so this may not come up until a later stage. When it does, the word "sharp" is the specific troublemaker: "D sharp" and "G sharp" are both commonly heard as "T-shirt", and because either one could have produced that, Nofret offers you both and lets you tap the one you meant rather than guessing at it. If you keep hitting that, try saying the flat name instead — "E flat" for D♯, "A flat" for G♯. Nofret accepts either spelling whatever your notation setting says.

The setting lives in Settings › Sound and input › Answer by voice mode. Turning it on asks for microphone permission and switches off the guitar sound, so playback can't be mistaken for your answer. Nofret also learns your pronunciation over time; Reset voice adaptation in that section clears what it has learned.

Can I use a different tuning?

Yes — Settings › Notation › Tuning offers Drop D and Open G alongside Standard, and they're part of Pro. Each tuning keeps its own stage, its own history and its own progress, because a note you know cold in Standard is somewhere else entirely in Drop D. Switching tunings never disturbs what you've built in another one.

I paid and it's not unlocked.

Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases is the first thing to try, especially on a new phone. If that doesn't do it, email me with the Apple Account email you purchased with and I'll sort it out.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Nofret. Apple handles cancellations, so it isn't something I can do from my end. If you bought Pro as the one-time purchase rather than the subscription, there is nothing to cancel — it doesn't renew. Full detail on the Terms page.

Will my progress sync to my other devices?

Not yet. Everything is stored locally on each device for now.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything. Inside the app, Settings › Progress bypass has Reset <tuning> progress for one tuning and Erase all progress for the lot. Nothing is stored anywhere but your phone, so there's nothing for me to delete on your behalf — see the Privacy Policy.
