The app

Nofret is a guitar fretboard trainer for iPhone, made by Pierce Boyne, an independent developer. Downloading and using the free portion of the app costs nothing and requires no account.

Nofret Pro

Nofret Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that unlocks the full curriculum and the additional practice modes. The exact price and subscription length are shown in the app before you buy, and in the App Store listing, in your local currency.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused portion of it is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from your iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then Nofret. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling is handled by Apple; there is no way for me to cancel a subscription on your behalf, and no cancellation fee.

Refunds

Refunds are issued by Apple, not by me. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. I have no access to your payment method and cannot issue a refund directly — but if something in the app is broken, tell me and I will fix it.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone, use Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases inside Nofret. Your subscription follows your Apple Account, not the device.

What Nofret is not

Nofret is a practice tool. It is provided as-is, without warranty that it will suit any particular purpose or that it will be free of bugs. It is not music tuition, and nothing in it constitutes professional advice. Play within your comfort — if practising causes pain in your hands or wrists, stop and see a doctor.
