The app

Nofret is a guitar fretboard trainer for iPhone, made by Pierce Boyne, an independent developer. Downloading and using the free portion of the app costs nothing and requires no account.

Nofret Pro

There are two ways to buy Pro, and both unlock exactly the same thing: the full nine-stage curriculum, the additional practice modes, and the alternate tunings. Prices are shown in the app before you buy, and in the App Store listing, in your local currency.

A one-time purchase

You pay once and keep Pro permanently. Nothing renews, nothing expires, and there is nothing to cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.

A yearly subscription

An auto-renewing subscription, offered with a 7-day free trial, if you would rather try Pro before committing to it.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial if you took one.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused portion of it is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Canceling

This applies to the yearly subscription only — the one-time purchase does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

Cancel any time from your iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then Nofret. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for. Canceling is handled by Apple; there is no way for me to cancel a subscription on your behalf, and no cancellation fee.

Refunds

Refunds are issued by Apple, not by me. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. I have no access to your payment method and cannot issue a refund directly — but if something in the app is broken, tell me and I will fix it.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone, use Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases inside Nofret. Your purchase follows your Apple Account, not the device, whichever of the two you bought.

What Nofret is not

Nofret is a practice tool. It is provided as-is, without warranty that it will suit any particular purpose or that it will be free of bugs. It is not music tuition, and nothing in it constitutes professional advice. Play within your comfort — if practicing causes pain in your hands or wrists, stop and see a doctor.
